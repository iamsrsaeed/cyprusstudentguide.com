## 2. Documents Required for the Student Motion Card

To get your plastic personalized smart card (Motion Card), you cannot buy it directly from the bus driver. You must visit the central ticketing office in your district with the following documents:

1. **Original Passport** (plus your ARC / White Slip or Pink Slip receipt if already issued).
2. **Valid Student ID Card** or an official **Certificate of Registration / Acceptance Letter** from your university.
3. **Passport-size photo** (at major central stations like EMEL Limassol or CPT Nicosia, desk staff take your digital photo on the spot via webcam).
4. **Card Issuance Fee:** €5 (one-time fee for issuing and encoding the personalized smart plastic card).

## 4. Bus Operators Across Cyprus

Each district operates its urban lines independently under the unified national smart card system:

* **Limassol:** EMEL (*Limassol Buses* - Blue buses)
* **Nicosia & Larnaca:** CPT (*Cyprus Public Transport*)
* **Paphos:** OSYPA (*Pafos Buses*)
* **Famagusta / Ayia Napa:** OSEA (*Famagusta Buses*)
* **Intercity Travel:** *Intercity Buses Cyprus* (Green long-distance highway coaches)

---

## 5. How to Top Up & Use Your Card

1. **Tap at the Driver's Console:** Always board through the front door and tap your plastic smart card against the electronic validator reader next to the driver. Wait for the green confirmation beep.
2. **Recharge Options:** You can load your monthly pass or cash wallet directly at central bus stations or online via the official transport web portal.
3. **Wallet vs. Period Pass:** If you only travel 2–3 times a week, keep €10 in your e-wallet balance and pay per ride at €0.75. If you travel daily, activate the monthly unlimited pass (€22.50 in Limassol, €20.00 in Nicosia/Larnaca).
4. **Motion Bus Card Mobile App:** You can link your physical card number inside the official *Motion Cyprus* mobile app to check live wallet balances and monitor real-time bus arrivals at your local stop.

---

## 6. Practical Survival Tips for Students

* **Always Carry Your Student ID:** Ticket inspectors perform random on-bus audits. Even with an active student Motion Card, they may ask to see your university ID to verify valid student status.
* **Weekend & Night Schedules:** Most regular urban schedules reduce frequency significantly after 8:30 PM. Night buses operate on limited key routes with a slight night tariff.
* **Intercity Escapes:** The green **Intercity Buses** connect Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, and Nicosia via highway routes. With your student discount, a single journey costs only €2.00 to €2.50—making weekend travel extremely affordable.
* **Airport Shuttles Note:** Private airport express buses (such as Limassol Airport Express and Kapnos Shuttle) operate independently from the public bus network and do not accept the 50% public student discount.
