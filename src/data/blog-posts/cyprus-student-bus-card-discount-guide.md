Public transportation is by far the most cost-effective way for international students to get around Cyprus. Whether commuting daily to your university campus in Limassol, Nicosia, or Larnaca, or traveling between cities on weekends, having a **Personalized Student Motion Card** cuts your travel expenses by **50%**.

In this guide, we break down exactly how to apply for your student bus card, district-specific fares (including Limassol's updated rates), and essential travel tips.

---

## 1. The 50% Student Discount & Real Fare Breakdown

In the Republic of Cyprus, registered university and college students holding a valid student ID are entitled to a **50% discount** on all public bus fares across urban, suburban, and intercity routes.

### District & Route Fare Comparison

| Ticket Type | Standard Public Fare | Student Fare (50% Off) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Single Urban Ride (Day)** | €1.50 – €2.00 | **€0.75 – €1.00** |
| **Daily Unlimited Urban Pass** | €5.00 | **€2.50** |
| **Monthly Urban Pass (Limassol - EMEL)** | €45.00 | **€22.50** |
| **Monthly Urban Pass (Nicosia / Larnaca - CPT)** | €40.00 | **€20.00** |
| **Intercity Single (e.g., Limassol to Nicosia / Larnaca)** | €4.00 – €5.00 | **€2.00 – €2.50** |
| **Monthly Intercity Pass** | €70.00 | **€35.00** |

*Pro Tip: If you take the bus daily to university, work, or grocery runs in Limassol, getting the **€22.50 Monthly Student Pass** on your plastic Motion Card pays for itself in less than two weeks.*

---

## 2. Documents Required for the Student Motion Card

To get your plastic personalized smart card (Motion Card), visit the central bus station in your city (e.g., **EMEL Central Station at Leontiou / Old Port in Limassol**, or **CPT Solomos Square in Nicosia**).

Bring the following documents:
1. **Original Passport** (and your ARC / Pink Slip if already issued).
2. **Valid Student ID Card** or an official **Certificate of Registration / Acceptance Letter** from your university.
3. **Passport-size photo** (at major central stations, staff can capture your photo digitally on the spot).
4. **Card Issue Fee**: €5 (one-time fee for issuing the smart plastic card).

---

## 3. Bus Operators Across Cyprus

Each district operates under the unified national smart card system:

* **Limassol:** EMEL (*Limassol Buses* - Blue buses)
* **Nicosia & Larnaca:** CPT (*Cyprus Public Transport*)
* **Paphos:** OSYPA (*Pafos Buses*)
* **Famagusta / Ayia Napa:** OSEA (*Famagusta Buses*)
* **Intercity Travel:** *Intercity Buses Cyprus* (Green long-distance buses)

---

## 4. How to Top Up & Use Your Card

1. **Tap at the Driver's Console:** When boarding from the front door, tap your plastic smart card against the validator machine next to the driver.
2. **Recharge Options:** You can load your monthly pass or cash wallet directly at central bus stations or online via the official transport web portal.
3. **Wallet vs. Period Pass:** If you only travel 2–3 times a week, keep €10 in your e-wallet and pay per ride at €0.75. If you travel daily, activate the monthly unlimited pass (€22.50 in Limassol).

---

## 5. Practical Survival Tips for Students

* **Always carry your physical Student ID** along with your Motion Card; inspectors conduct random checks and will ask for your university ID.
* **Weekend & Night Routes:** Regular schedules reduce frequency after 8:30 PM. Night buses operate on select routes with a slight night surcharge.
* **Intercity Weekend Escapes:** Use the green **Intercity Buses** for traveling between Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, and Nicosia—with your student discount, single tickets cost only €2.00 to €2.50.
