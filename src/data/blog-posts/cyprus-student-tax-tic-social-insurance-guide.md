Securing legal part-time employment in Cyprus—whether with hospitality venues, distribution warehouses, or food delivery courier platforms (Wolt, Foody)—requires two fundamental government registration numbers: a **Tax Identification Code (TIC)** and a **Social Insurance Number (SIS)**.

Without these registrations, employers cannot legally submit your tripartite student employment contract to the District Labour Office, payroll deductions cannot be processed, and you will be completely locked out of Cyprus's free national healthcare system (**GeSY**).

In this practical 2026 procedural guide, we explain how to get your **TIC through the TFA portal**, how to obtain your **Social Insurance Number**, the exact **monthly salary deductions**, and how to activate your **GeSY healthcare coverage**.

## 1. What is the Tax Identification Code (TIC) and How to Apply?

The **Tax Identification Code (TIC)**—formerly known as the AFM—is issued by the Cyprus Tax Department (Department of Taxation). It serves as your unique tax identity throughout your residence in the Republic.

## 2. Income Tax Exemption Threshold (€19,500 Rule)

A widespread concern among newly employed international students is whether high personal income taxes will diminish their hourly earnings.

> 💰 **The €19,500 Tax-Free Allowance:** In the Republic of Cyprus, personal income up to **€19,500 per calendar year is taxed at 0%**. 

Because Non-EU students are legally restricted to working a maximum of 20 hours per week during term-time, annual student earnings typically range between €5,500 and €8,500. Consequently, **students pay zero personal income tax**. 

You are, however, still legally obligated to register for a TIC to permit statutory social contributions.

## 4. Free Healthcare: Linking Your Contributions to GeSY

Once your employer starts remitting your monthly Social Insurance and GeSY contributions, you unlock full access to the **General Healthcare System (GeSY)**:
1. **Visit the GeSY Portal:** Navigate to `gesy.org.cy` and select Beneficiary Registration.
2. **Identity Verification:** Enter your ARC number and Social Insurance Number (SIS).
3. **Choose a Personal Doctor (GP):** Select an English-speaking general practitioner near your residence in Limassol, Nicosia, Larnaca, or Paphos.
4. **Free Care Access:** Doctor visits, medical specialist referrals, hospital emergency care, and prescription medicines become fully subsidized (prescription medicines typically cost just €1.00 per item at any pharmacy).

## 5. Practical Checklist for Food Delivery Couriers (Wolt / Foody)

* **Fleet Partner Documentation:** If working through a fleet partner agency, ensure the agency provides monthly payment slips explicitly itemizing your Social Insurance contributions.
* **Annual Tax Return Submission:** All TIC holders in Cyprus must submit an annual personal income tax declaration online via the TFA portal before the statutory autumn deadline, even when declaring income below the €19,500 threshold.
