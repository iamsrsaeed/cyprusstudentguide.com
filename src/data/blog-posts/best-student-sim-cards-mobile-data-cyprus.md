Staying connected without overpaying is one of the very first priorities when landing in Cyprus. While free Wi-Fi is common in university campuses and cafes, having reliable 4G/5G mobile data is essential for bus schedules, grocery apps, part-time shifts, and navigation.

In this guide, we compare the top telecom networks in Cyprus and break down the cheapest prepaid plans tailored for international students.

---

## 1. The Major Telecom Providers in Cyprus

There are four primary telecom operators across the Republic of Cyprus:

1. **Cyta (soeasy):** The state-backed operator. It offers the strongest and widest network coverage across cities, mountain villages, and coastal highways, though prices are slightly higher.
2. **Epic:** Highly popular among students and youth. Known for aggressive unlimited data offers and competitive prepaid top-up bonuses.
3. **Cablenet (Purple):** Fastest-growing provider with highly competitive mobile packages and budget-friendly prepaid deals.
4. **PrimeTel:** Offers flexible prepaid plans and regular double-data promotions.

---

## 2. Best Prepaid Plans for Students (Pay-As-You-Go)

Most international students start with a **Prepaid (Pay-As-You-Go)** SIM card rather than signing a 24-month contract, especially before receiving their ARC (Pink Slip).

### Popular Student Bundle Breakdown

| Provider | Package / Bundle | Monthly Cost | Included Data & Perks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cyta (soeasy)** | soeasy Talk & Surf | €10.00 – €15.00 | 10GB – 25GB + Unlimited local minutes & SMS |
| **Epic** | Epic BB (Big Bundle) | €10.00 – €12.00 | 15GB – 30GB (Frequent seasonal unlimited promos) |
| **Cablenet** | Purple Max Mobile Prep | €10.00 | 20GB + Local minutes |
| **PrimeTel** | Top-Up Bonus Bundle | €10.00 | 12GB – 20GB |

*Tip: Epic and Cyta run frequent in-app promo top-ups where €10–€15 top-ups yield double data or weekend unlimited packs.*

## 3. How to Buy and Register a SIM Card

Cyprus law mandates identity registration for all SIM cards (including prepaid).

### Documents Needed:
1. **Original Passport**
2. **Physical Address / Hotel / Dorm Address in Cyprus**

### Where to Buy:
* **Airport Kiosks / Periptero:** You can grab a starter pack (€2 – €5) immediately upon arrival at Larnaca or Paphos airport.
* **Official Provider Stores:** Visiting an official Cyta, Epic, or Cablenet store in city centers (e.g., Anexartisias in Limassol or Ledras in Nicosia) is recommended, as staff will activate and register the SIM on the spot.

---

## 4. eSIM vs. Physical SIM for New Arrivals

If your smartphone supports eSIM, you can install an international data eSIM (via Airalo, Maya Mobile, or Nomad) before boarding your flight. This ensures you have instant maps and messaging connectivity the minute you touch down in Cyprus, after which you can switch to a local physical SIM for daily local calling and texting.

---

## 5. Money-Saving Tips for Students

* **Use Provider Apps:** Download the **My Cyta** or **Epic Cyprus** app to top up online using your debit card; in-app recharges frequently give free extra gigabytes.
* **Avoid Airport Long-Term Traps:** Do not buy overpriced "Tourist Data Packages" at airport arrival gates. Buy a basic €2 starter pack and activate standard monthly student bundles instead.
