Finding part-time work in Cyprus as an international student from outside the EU (Non-EU) comes with strict legal boundaries set by the Ministry of Labour, Welfare and Social Insurance. While working helps offset living expenses, failing to comply with immigration rules can lead to visa cancellation.

**Quick Summary for Non-EU Student Workers in Cyprus**

| Requirement | Legal Provision / Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly Hour Limit** | Maximum **20 hours/week** during semesters; up to **38 hours/week** during summer breaks. |
| **Eligibility Timeline** | Allowed to work after completing **6 consecutive months** of study in Cyprus. |
| **Permitted Sectors** | Food delivery, restaurant staff, agriculture, cleaning, petrol stations, car washes. |
| **Mandatory Document** | Signed **Employment Contract** approved by the District Labour Office. |
| **Social Insurance** | Employer must register you for a **Social Insurance (TIC/SI)** contribution number. |

## 1. The Legal Framework: Key Regulations

The Cyprus Ministry of Labour restricts Non-EU students to specific sectors to protect domestic employment.

### Eligible Work Sectors
Non-EU international students holding a valid temporary residence permit (Pink Slip) are permitted to work in:
* **Trade & Hospitality:** Kitchen assistants, dishwashers, food delivery riders, cleaners, fast-food crew.
* **Services:** Car washes, petrol station attendants, warehouse loaders.
* **Agriculture & Animal Husbandry:** Farm workers and processing plant helpers.

> **Important Legal Rule:** Non-EU students are generally **not permitted** to work in retail cashier roles, administrative office jobs, or customer-facing reception desks unless special bilateral agreements exist.

## 2. Essential Documents Needed Before Starting Work

Before taking your first shift, ensure the following paperwork is submitted to the **District Labour Office (Tmima Ergasias)**:

1. **Valid Passport & Pink Slip (or Pink Slip Application Receipt / Fiche).**
2. **University Enrollment Certificate:** Confirming full-time student status and class schedule.
3. **Standard Employment Contract Form:** Signed by both the student and the employer, specifying maximum 20 working hours per week.
4. **Social Insurance Registration:** Proof that your employer has registered your work activity.

## 3. Real-World Tips: The "Unspoken" Job Market

While official guides recommend submitting CVs online, international students in Cyprus find jobs most effectively through specific local channels:

* **The Senior Referral Pipeline:** Most restaurant and delivery jobs are handed down from graduating or departing students to their network. Join active student WhatsApp and Telegram communities in your city (Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, or Paphos).
* **Delivery Platforms:** Food delivery (Wolt, Foody) requires a valid motorbike or car driver's license and a legal contract via an authorized fleet manager or direct agency.
* **Timing the Agricultural & Summer Shifts:** During seasonal breaks, legal hours increase to 38 hours per week, allowing students to earn significantly more during peak tourist months.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Can I work cash-in-hand without a contract?**  
Working without an approved Ministry of Labour contract and social insurance is illegal. Cyprus immigration authorities conduct regular spot checks, and unregistered work risks immediate deportation.

**What is the minimum wage for student jobs in Cyprus?**  
Cyprus enforces a national minimum wage for specific sectors. For standard hospitality and service roles, net pay generally ranges between **€5.00 to €7.50 per hour**, depending on tips, shift timings, and duties.
