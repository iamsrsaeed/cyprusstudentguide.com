Stepping off the plane as a new student in Cyprus is exciting, but the very first hour inside **Larnaca International Airport (LCA)** or **Paphos Airport (PFO)** can catch you off guard if you aren't prepared with local euro cash and change.

Here is a practical, ground-tested breakdown of what to expect right after baggage reclaim, the luggage trolley coin mechanism, and how to navigate without unnecessary stress.

---

## 1. The €2 Baggage Trolley Mechanism

Inside both Larnaca and Paphos baggage claim halls, luggage trolleys/carts are chained together with mechanical coin locks.

* **The Requirement:** You need a **€2 coin** (or in some locks, a **€1 coin**) to release a trolley from the bay.
* **Refundable Deposit:** When you return and re-chain the trolley into a bay at the arrivals exit or outside parking, your coin automatically pops back out.
* **The Common Trap:** Most new international arrivals only carry €50 or €100 euro banknotes from foreign exchange booths back home. Having heavy 25–40kg baggage without a single metal coin makes moving through customs exhausting.

---

## 2. How to Get Coins Inside the Airport

If you did not bring small Euro coins from home or transit hubs, here are your options inside the terminal:

### Option A: Coin Change Machines
There are automated banknote-to-coin change machines situated near the baggage carousel areas. 
* **Note on Limitations:** These machines typically accept only **€5, €10, or occasionally €20 notes**. They frequently reject wrinkled bills, larger €50/€100 notes, or foreign currencies.

### Option B: Foreign Exchange Desks (Travelex / Currency Exchange)
* If the automated change machines are out of order or you only hold large banknotes, approach the currency exchange counters in the arrivals hall.
* Politely ask the teller to break a small note into €2 coins. If they are not busy with foreign transfers, they will usually assist travelers with change.

### Option C: Airport Duty-Free or Kiosk Purchase
* Buy a small bottle of water or chewing gum at the arrivals snack bar/duty-free shop using a small banknote to get coins back in your change.

## 3. Immediate Checklist After Grabbing Your Bags

| Step | Action | Practical Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Free Airport Wi-Fi** | Connect to "Hermes Airports Free Wi-Fi" | Quick login via web browser to message your university coordinator or driver. |
| **2. Local SIM Starter** | Grab a prepaid SIM at arrivals kiosk | Avoid overpriced long-term packs; buy a simple €2–€5 prepaid pack. |
| **3. Water & Snacks** | Stay hydrated | Cyprus summer temperatures are high; grab water before leaving the air-conditioned hall. |
| **4. Boarding Transport** | Head to official bus/shuttle bays | Follow painted pedestrian signs directly to Limassol Airport Express, Kapnos Shuttle, or city buses. |

---

## 4. Summary & Pro Tip for Incoming Students

If possible, exchange at least one **€2 coin** or a couple of **€5 notes** during your transit airport layover (e.g., in Dubai, Doha, Athens, or Istanbul) before boarding your final flight to Cyprus. Having loose coins ready in your pocket lets you grab a trolley instantly and breeze through arrivals without waiting around change machines!
