## 1. The Baggage Trolley Coin Problem & Easy Hacks

The first practical obstacle international students face at baggage reclaim is the mechanical trolley locking mechanism:
* **The Lock System:** Luggage carts at Larnaca and Paphos airports are chained together with mechanical key slots. Inserting a **€1.00 or €2.00 coin** into the slot pops the chain lock open.
* **Why Students Get Stuck:** Newly arriving third-country students typically hold foreign currency, large €50/€100 banknotes, or digital cards—none of which fit into the mechanical slot.
* **The Solution:** 
  1. **Change Machines:** Change machines dispensing €1 and €2 coins are located near the luggage carousels, accepting €5, €10, and €20 notes.
  2. **Duty-Free / Café Purchase:** Purchase a bottle of water or chewing gum at the airside arrivals store to break a paper note into physical coins.
  3. **The Key / Card Hack:** If you have an old metal house key or sturdy token with a round head matching the exact diameter of a €1 coin, inserting the rounded top can compress the internal release latch. When finished, re-dock the cart into another trolley to retrieve your coin.

### Transit Operators Breakdown
1. **To Limassol:** Take the **Limassol Airport Express** coach stationed directly outside departures/arrivals. Ticket counters are inside the arrivals hall. The bus drops passengers at Agios Georgios Havouzas terminal or the Tourist Area.
2. **To Nicosia:** Board the **Kapnos Airport Shuttle**. Comfortable air-conditioned coaches depart roughly every 45 to 60 minutes directly to the main Kapnos station in Nicosia.
3. **To Larnaca City Centre:** Do not hire a taxi for a 10-minute ride. Walk to the upper departures road stop and hop on **CPT Public Bus 425**, which drops you at the Finikoudes promenade for just **€2.00 to €2.40** (or €1.00–€1.20 if student Motion profile is applied).

## 3. Border Immigration Protocols for Third-Country Students

Cyprus border police at Larnaca and Paphos checkpoints inspect incoming student documents strictly:
* Keep all paperwork in your **cabin hand luggage** (never in checked baggage).
* Keep ready:
  * Original Passport with valid Entry Visa / Entry Clearance.
  * University Enrolment Letter & Tuition Payment Receipts.
  * Medical Blood/X-ray test certificates from your home country.
  * Proof of temporary or permanent accommodation address in Cyprus.
  * Minimum €1,000–€2,000 equivalent in cash, international cards, or sponsor guarantee.
