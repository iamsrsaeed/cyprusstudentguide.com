For international students in Cyprus, **Lidl** is the primary destination for affordable groceries, pantry staples, and fresh dairy products. 

However, simply scanning the **Lidl Plus app** without understanding its activation rules means leaving valuable savings on the table. In Cyprus, **all discounts—whether weekly item deals, Shop & Win milestones, or scratch bonuses—require manual activation before checkout.**

Here is a practical, step-by-step walkthrough on setting up the app correctly with a Cyprus store profile, understanding the activation rules, and unlocking every monthly reward tier.

---

## 1. Setting Up the App Correctly in Cyprus

Many students encounter errors when installing Lidl Plus if their Google Play Store or Apple App Store is still linked to their home country.

* **Download the App:** Search for `Lidl Plus` on the Play Store or App Store.
* **Select the Country/Region:** When prompted during the initial setup, **always select Cyprus**. If you select your home country or a different EU state, your digital card barcode will fail at Cyprus cash registers.
* **Choose Your Local Store:** Select your nearest branch (e.g., Lemesos/Agios Athanasios, Nicosia, Larnaca, or Paphos) to view store opening hours and localized leaflets.
* **Phone Verification:** You can register using your local Cypriot mobile number (Cyta, Epic, PrimeTel) to receive SMS authentication.

---

## 2. The Golden Rule: Manual Activation is Mandatory for EVERYTHING

The single most common reason students fail to get their discounts is expecting the app to apply rewards automatically upon scanning.

> ⚠️ **The Golden Rule:** Lidl Plus **never** applies discounts automatically. **EVERY coupon you wish to use—including all financial discount vouchers, weekly product markdowns, and scratch card rewards—MUST be manually tapped and activated in the "Coupons" tab BEFORE the cashier scans your card.**

### Understanding the Navigation Tabs:
* **Coupons Tab (Requires Manual Activation):** Contains your active product offers, unlocked milestone vouchers, and scratch prizes. You must tap **"ACTIVATE"** on each coupon you want to redeem during your shop.
* **Savers Tab:** General in-store catalogue promotions and weekly specials that apply directly to shelf prices.
* **Leaflets Tab:** Digital weekly flyers showing upcoming Monday and Thursday promotions.

---

## 3. Unlocking Monthly Spend Milestones: "Shop & Win!"

Lidl Cyprus runs an official monthly loyalty tracker called **Shop & Win!** right on the app home screen. Every time your Lidl Plus card QR code is scanned, your cumulative monthly spend across physical stores in Cyprus moves the progress bar forward.

The progress bar unlocks **4 direct monetary discount vouchers**:

| Accumulated Purchases | Unlocked Voucher Reward | Redemption Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **€60.00** | **1€ Discount!** | Sent to *Coupons* ➜ **Must tap ACTIVATE** before next shop |
| **€100.00** | **1.5€ Discount!** | Sent to *Coupons* ➜ **Must tap ACTIVATE** before next shop |
| **€170.00** | **2€ Discount!** | Sent to *Coupons* ➜ **Must tap ACTIVATE** before next shop |
| **€300.00** | **4€ Discount!** | Sent to *Coupons* ➜ **Must tap ACTIVATE** before next shop |

### Key Campaign Terms:
* **Monthly Reset:** The milestone tracker runs for each calendar month and shows a live countdown (e.g., *16 days left*). It resets automatically at **00:00 on the 1st of every month**.
* **Excluded Items:** Spending on the following items does **not** count toward your spend total:
  1. Tobacco products
  2. Baby food
  3. Pharmaceuticals / vitamins / OTC products
  4. Prepaid third-party cards
  5. Shopping bags (small & large)
  6. Newspapers & magazines

## 4. Digital Scratch Cards (Scratch & Win)

After completing a qualifying scanned purchase at the register:
* A digital scratch card pops up on the app home screen.
* Scratching the card reveals instant rewards—ranging from monetary discount vouchers (e.g., **€0.50 off**) to free bakery and grocery items.
* **Important:** Just like milestone vouchers, scratch card rewards are placed directly into your **Coupons** tab. You **must tap ACTIVATE** on the reward before your next checkout for the discount to deduct from your total.

---

## 5. Practical Checklist for the Cash Register

To ensure a seamless, hassle-free checkout at the counter:

1. **Activate Everything in Advance:** Supermarket cellular signals can sometimes be weak indoors. Open the app on Wi-Fi before entering the store and tap **ACTIVATE** on every coupon, Shop & Win voucher, and scratch bonus you plan to use.
2. **Open Your Digital Card:** Tap the blue floating **Lidl Plus Card** button at the bottom of the screen to open your personal QR code.
3. **Scan at the Terminal:** Place the QR code facing down on the optical scanner beside the card payment terminal before the cashier finishes scanning your groceries.
4. **Verify on the Receipt:** The physical or in-app digital receipt will list every activated coupon deduction item-by-item.
