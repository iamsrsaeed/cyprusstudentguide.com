## 1. The Golden Rule: Manual Activation is Mandatory

The single most common mistake international students make at Lidl cash registers across Limassol, Nicosia, Larnaca, and Paphos is expecting shelf promotions to apply automatically upon scanning their app barcode.

> ⚠️ **The Golden Rule:** Lidl Plus **NEVER** applies coupon discounts automatically. Every weekly offer, category markdown, and milestone money voucher **MUST be manually tapped and marked as "ACTIVE"** in the "Coupons" tab *before* your card barcode is scanned by the cashier.

### Understanding the 3 In-App Promotional Sections
1. **Coupons Tab (Requires Manual Tap):** Single-use weekly percentage markdowns (e.g., 20% off Milbona dairy, €1 off bakery items). Must click **"ACTIVATE"**.
2. **Savers Tab (Shelf Discounts):** Regular catalogue sales available to all shoppers; shelf prices automatically reflect these deals without manual clicks.
3. **Leaflets Tab (Weekly Previews):** Digital PDF flyers detailing Monday and Thursday promotional product releases.

### Critical Spend Accumulation Rules
* **Where vouchers appear:** Once you reach a spend threshold (e.g., €60), the €1.00 voucher is sent to your **"Coupons"** tab. You must tap **ACTIVATE** before your next shop to deduct it from your receipt.
* **Excluded Items:** Spending on tobacco, infant formula, newspapers/magazines, plastic shopping bags, and gift cards does not count toward your spend total.
* **Calendar Reset:** The spend tracker resets to €0.00 at 00:00 on the 1st day of every month. Vouchers unlocked in the final days of the month typically carry a 7-day validity window.

## 3. Digital Scratch Cards ("Ξύνεις Κερδίζεις")

Every completed checkout where your Lidl Plus card is scanned automatically awards a digital **Scratch Card** inside the app within 5 to 15 minutes of payment:
* Swipe your finger across the scratch area to reveal a free item or a heavy category discount (e.g., free chocolate bar, 30% off Freshona canned beans, or €0.50 off orange juice).
* Scratch card prizes expire within **3 to 7 days**, so activate them right away before your next grocery run.

## 4. Practical Supermarket Hacks for Student Housemates

1. **The Shared Flat Loyalty Hack:** If you share a flat with 2–4 housemates, do your weekly supermarket runs together and **scan one single flatmate's Lidl Plus card**. Consolidating purchases easily pushes the account past the €170 and €300 milestone thresholds, unlocking the maximum €4 cash vouchers that single students struggle to reach alone.
2. **Weekly Monday & Thursday Cycle:** Lidl rolls out new fresh product promotions every Monday morning and household/non-food specials every Thursday morning. New app coupons refresh every Monday.
3. **Check the Digital Receipt:** No need to save paper receipts for apartment expense splitting. The app saves full itemized digital receipts under **"More ➔ Purchase History"**, which can be exported directly.
