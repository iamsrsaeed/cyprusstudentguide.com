Under the official regulations of the **Civil Registry and Migration Department (CRMD)** of the Republic of Cyprus, every first-time non-EU international student must undergo a mandatory health screening to obtain their **Temporary Residence Permit (Pink Slip / ARC)**.

These certified medical test results are **valid for 3 months** from the date of issue and must be submitted along with your Plan A private health insurance.

Here is the exact breakdown of all 5 mandatory tests, university package inclusions, city-by-city private costs, and step-by-step procedures.

---

## 1. The 5 Mandatory Medical Tests Required by CRMD

The official immigration checklist requires screening for infectious diseases through **4 blood tests and 1 radiological scan**:

| Test No. | Examination Name | Target Condition / Marker | Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **HIV Test** | Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV 1 & 2) | Blood Sample |
| **2** | **Hepatitis B** | Hepatitis B Surface Antigen (HBsAg) | Blood Sample |
| **3** | **Hepatitis C** | Hepatitis C Antibody (Anti-HCV) | Blood Sample |
| **4** | **Syphilis** | Treponemal Disease Screening (VDRL / RPR) | Blood Sample |
| **5** | **Tuberculosis (TB)** | Active Pulmonary Tuberculosis | **Chest X-Ray (PA View)** |

*Important:* The Chest X-ray must be examined and signed by a licensed radiologist/pulmonologist in Cyprus confirming clear lung fields.

---

## 2. Check First: Is Medical & Insurance Included in Your Tuition?

Before paying money out of pocket at private diagnostic centers, **always verify your invoice with your university's International Student Office**:

* **All-in-One University Packages:** Many private universities and colleges include **both the complete medical examination and 1-year private health insurance** directly inside your initial admission fee or tuition deposit.
* **Campus Screening Days:** Institutions that bundle this service arrange specialized campus screening sessions where mobile lab teams collect blood samples and transport students to partner radiology centers at **no extra charge**.
* **Direct Issuance:** Your institution will issue your stamped **Certificate of Health Insurance (Plan A)** and include your sealed test results directly into your migration submission file.

## 4. Student Private Health Insurance (Plan A)

Non-EU international students do not qualify for free national healthcare (GeSY) and must hold an approved private **Plan A** immigration policy meeting CRMD minimums:

* **Inpatient Hospitalization:** Minimum €8,500 – €10,000 cover.
* **Outpatient Care & Medicine:** €1,000 – €1,750 cover.
* **Medical Repatriation:** Minimum €3,500 cover.
* **Certificate Requirement:** The stamped original policy certificate must be lodged alongside your medical results.

---

## 5. Step-by-Step Execution Checklist

1. **Confirm University Package:** Check if fees are pre-paid in your college tuition invoice.
2. **Bring Original Passport:** Diagnostic labs will not draw blood or perform X-rays without inspecting your physical passport and entry visa.
3. **Blood Draw & Chest X-Ray:** Complete the 4 blood tests and chest radiograph at an accredited lab/radiology center.
4. **Collect Sealed Doctor's Certificate:** Results typically take **24 to 48 hours**. Ensure the attending Cyprus doctor signs and stamps the final official migration medical report.
5. **Lodge Within 3 Months:** Submit the medical certificate, insurance policy, and bank statement to your student affairs office to book your biometrics appointment.

---

## 6. Pro Tips

* **Validity Period:** All medical results are strictly **valid for 3 months (90 days)** from the test date. Do not do them too early if your bank account or tenancy contract is delayed.
* **One-Time Obligation:** The full medical exam is only required for your **first-time Pink Slip application**. Annual renewals do not require repeating these medical tests unless specifically requested.
