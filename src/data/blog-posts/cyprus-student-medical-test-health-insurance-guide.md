Completing mandatory medical screening and securing an approved private health insurance policy is one of the very first legal requirements every Non-EU international student must fulfill upon arrival in Cyprus. 

Without a clean government-certified medical clearance report and a valid immigration health insurance certificate, the **Civil Registry and Migration Department (CRMD)** will immediately reject your **Temporary Residence Permit (Pink Slip / MVIS)** application.

In this practical 2026 procedural guide, we explain the **exact 5 tests required**, **public vs. private clinic pricing**, **how to get X-ray reports certified**, and **which health insurance plans satisfy immigration standards**.

### Validity Window of Medical Certificates
* Test certificates for initial Pink Slip applications are valid for **3 months** from the date of issuance.
* **First-Time Gate:** The comprehensive medical test is mandatory for your initial first-year residence application in Cyprus. For standard subsequent annual student permit renewals, you are generally exempt from repeating the full medical exam unless your legal residence was interrupted.

## 4. Key Practical Traps to Avoid

1. **Travel Insurance is NOT Accepted:** Many students arrive with 30-day generic travel insurance purchased online. The Migration Department strictly rejects travel insurance policies—you must purchase a recognized Cypriot immigration medical policy.
2. **Name Mismatch on Lab Sheets:** Ensure your laboratory prints your full name and passport number **exactly as they appear in your international passport**. Even a slight typo will cause the immigration officer to reject the health certificate.
3. **Keep Original Blue-Ink Reports:** Migration authorities require original lab result documents with ink stamps and wet signatures; unverified black-and-white photocopies will not be accepted.
